About me

I've always been drawn to patterns, logic and the search for cause-and-effect relationships. I enjoy looking beyond the result and understanding what drives it.
Working closely with business processes taught me that data is rarely just data. Behind every metric, KPI or trend is a story about customer behavior, business decisions and real-world outcomes. I enjoy connecting those pieces together and turning scattered information into clear explanations.

I don't see dashboards as the final result. I see them as a starting point for asking better questions.

Today I use Power BI, Excel, SQL, Python, Tableau and statistical analysis to explore business challenges and uncover patterns. I also use AI as a thinking partner to explore ideas, challenge assumptions and accelerate the analytical process.
